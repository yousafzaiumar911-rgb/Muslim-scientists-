Famous Muslim Scientists

📐

Al-Khwarizmi

c. 780 – c. 850

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi was a famous mathematician, astronomer and scholar. His writings played an important role in the development of algebra and mathematics.

Ibn al-Haytham

c. 965 – c. 1040

Ibn al-Haytham made important contributions to optics, light and vision. His experimental approach influenced the development of scientific investigation.

Ibn Sina, also known as Avicenna, was a physician and philosopher. His famous medical writings were studied for centuries.

Al-Razi

About This Website

Muslim Scientists History is an educational website about the history of scientific scholarship in the Islamic world.


The website introduces famous scholars and explains their fields of study and contributions in a simple way.


Our goal is to make the history of science interesting and easy to explore for students and visitors.
