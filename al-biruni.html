Who Was Al-Biruni?

Abu Rayhan Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Biruni was one of the great scholars of the medieval Muslim world. He worked in many fields including astronomy, mathematics, geography, history, mineralogy and pharmacology.

He was born near Kath in Khwarazm around 973 CE and spent much of his early life studying mathematics, astronomy, medicine and other sciences.

🌌 Astronomy

Astronomy was one of Al-Biruni's major areas of study. A large part of his surviving scientific work concerns astronomy, mathematics and mathematical geography.

His major astronomical work, Al-Qanun al-Masudi, discussed astronomical parameters, calculations and planetary positions.

📐 Mathematics

Al-Biruni applied mathematics to practical scientific problems. His work included trigonometry and calculations used in astronomy and geography.

He also worked with spherical trigonometry and developed mathematical methods for astronomical calculations.

🌍 Measuring the Earth

Al-Biruni developed a method for estimating the radius of the Earth using the height of a mountain and the angle of the horizon.

He carried out this method at Nandana in the Indian subcontinent. His work also included calculations concerning the length of a degree and the dimensions of the Earth.

🇮🇳 Research on India

Al-Biruni spent years studying Indian culture, languages, philosophy, religion, science and astronomy.

He learned Sanskrit and used information from Indian sources in his research. His famous work on India is commonly known as Kitab al-Hind.

📖 Kitab al-Hind

Kitab al-Hind is one of Al-Biruni's best-known works. It contains detailed observations and discussions about Indian traditions, philosophy, science, astronomy and religious ideas.

The work is an important historical source for studying medieval India and the intellectual traditions that Al-Biruni encountered there.

📚 Major Works

  • Kitab al-Hind
  • Al-Qanun al-Masudi
  • Kitab al-Tafhim
  • Chronology of Ancient Nations
  • Kitab al-Jamahir fi Ma'rifat al-Jawahir

🔬 Other Scientific Studies

Al-Biruni also wrote about minerals, pharmacology, geography, chronology and the history of religions. His surviving works show a remarkably wide range of scientific interests.

🌟 His Legacy

Al-Biruni's writings became an important source for the history of medieval science, astronomy, mathematics and geography.

His approach combined mathematical calculation, observation and the study of information from different cultures.
