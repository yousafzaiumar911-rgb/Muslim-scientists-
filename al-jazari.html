Who Was Al-Jazari?

Badi al-Zaman Abu al-Izz ibn Ismail ibn al-Razzaz al-Jazari was an engineer, inventor and mechanical engineer who lived in the Islamic world during the 12th and early 13th centuries.

He worked for the Artuqid rulers in Diyarbakir and became famous for designing and describing ingenious mechanical devices.
