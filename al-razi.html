Who Was Al-Razi?

Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Zakariya al-Razi, known in the Latin world as Rhazes, was a major physician and scholar of the medieval Islamic world.

He was born in Rayy, near present-day Tehran, and later worked in major medical centers including Rayy and Baghdad. His writings covered medicine as well as several other intellectual fields.

⚕️ Contributions to Medicine

Al-Razi emphasized clinical observation and recorded detailed observations from his medical practice. His medical writings combined earlier knowledge with his own observations and experiences.

He wrote extensively about diseases, treatments, medicines and medical practice.

📖 Al-Hawi

One of Al-Razi's most important works was Kitab al-Hawi fi al-Tibb, commonly known as The Comprehensive Book of Medicine.

The work brought together medical knowledge from earlier sources as well as Al-Razi's own clinical observations and case material.

🦠 Smallpox and Measles

Al-Razi wrote an important treatise concerning smallpox and measles. His discussion distinguished the two diseases and became influential in the history of medicine.

His writings on these diseases were later translated into European languages and circulated beyond the Islamic world.

🧪 Chemistry and Alchemy

Al-Razi also studied substances and processes associated with alchemy and early chemistry. His writings included discussions of materials, laboratory procedures and chemical equipment.

His work helped preserve and develop practical knowledge about substances and laboratory techniques during the medieval period.

🧠 Philosophy and Other Studies

Al-Razi was not limited to medicine. Historical sources attribute works to him on philosophy, natural sciences, mathematics, astronomy and other subjects.

More than 200 works are attributed to him, although many of his writings have not survived.

📚 Major Works

  • Al-Hawi fi al-Tibb – The Comprehensive Book of Medicine
  • Al-Mansuri fi al-Tibb – The Book of Mansur
  • Treatise on Smallpox and Measles
  • Kitab al-Tajarib – Book of Experiments

🌍 His Legacy

Al-Razi's medical writings influenced physicians in the Islamic world and were later translated into Latin and studied in Europe.

His emphasis on clinical observation and his extensive medical writings made him an important figure in the history of medieval medicine.
