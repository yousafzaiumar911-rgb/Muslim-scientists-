Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi

c. 780 – c. 850

Field: Mathematics, Astronomy and Geography

Known for: Important early work in algebra and mathematical methods.

Who was Al-Khwarizmi?

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi was a Persian Muslim scholar who worked in the Abbasid-era intellectual center of Baghdad. He is one of the important figures in the history of mathematics.

Contribution to Mathematics

Al-Khwarizmi wrote an influential mathematical work dealing with methods for solving equations. The title of this work is associated with the historical development of the word "algebra".

Contribution to Astronomy

He also worked on astronomy and produced astronomical tables. His scholarly work helped transmit and develop mathematical and astronomical knowledge in the medieval Islamic world.

Famous Work

One of his best-known mathematical works is commonly referred to as Kitab al-Jabr wa'l-Muqabala.

His Historical Importance

Al-Khwarizmi's writings became influential beyond the Islamic world through later translations and helped shape the development of mathematics in Europe and elsewhere.
